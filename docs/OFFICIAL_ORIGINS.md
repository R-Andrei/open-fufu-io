# Open Fufu — Canonical Official Origin Roster

## Status and authority

This document is the **canonical owner for Official Origin identities, display names, trait compositions, default/order choices, roster authoring guidance, and strategic-fantasy descriptions**.

Origin builder rules, trait mechanics, costs, refunds, and composition semantics are owned by [`ORIGIN_TRAIT_CATALOGUE.md`](./ORIGIN_TRAIT_CATALOGUE.md). Official AI preset pools are owned by [`official-ai/OFFICIAL_AI_PRESETS.md`](./official-ai/OFFICIAL_AI_PRESETS.md). Migration sequencing is owned by [`OPENFRONT_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md`](./OPENFRONT_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md).

The roster below is the accepted **provisional V1 Official Origin library**. Display names are the accepted V1 naming baseline and remain separable from stable Oxx content IDs.

Official Origins obey exactly the same public builder/catalogue rules as Custom Origins. No Official Origin receives hidden points, hidden traits, compatibility exceptions, or creator-only mechanics.

Trait IDs in the roster are authoritative composition data. Parenthetical mechanical phrases are **non-authoritative shorthand for readability**; the Pxx/Nxx definitions in `ORIGIN_TRAIT_CATALOGUE.md` own the actual mechanics and values.

## Default and authoring direction

**Business as Usual (O08)** is the provisional V1 **first-listed and default-selected Origin** in the player UI.

It exists for a player who does not yet want to solve an Origin buildcraft puzzle: it uses a small number of broadly useful effects and otherwise stays close to ordinary Open Fufu play.

Official-Origin authoring follows these content rules:

- **Simple does not mean intentionally weak.** A starter-friendly Origin should normally use roughly the ordinary base budget without forcing the player into several rule transformations.
- Leaving points or trait slots unused is legal and sometimes desirable.
- **9–10 positive spend is a healthy ordinary roster baseline.**
- An Official Origin with **8 or fewer positive points requires a specific content/design justification**; low complexity alone is not enough reason to leave a large fraction of the base budget unused.
- Specialized Origins may spend above the ordinary base budget by accepting coherent drawbacks within the public builder rules.
- Drawbacks for beginner-oriented Origins should prefer understandable scalar tradeoffs over mechanics that radically change the rules or demand specialized knowledge.
- Origins are a **global shared library**, not content owned by one AI character. Multiple AI presets may legally include the same Official Origin in their allowed pools even when the Origin name/reference has no lore relationship to that character.
- Duplicate mechanical builds under different presentation names are generally undesirable.

The first three UI-facing starter choices are:

| Origin | Compact build | Intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Business as Usual — DEFAULT** | **P06** · **P09** | Near-vanilla generalist. |
| **Head Start** | **P01** · **P06** · **N01** | Stronger opening position/trade with a mild long-term growth drawback. |
| **Home Field Advantage** | **P09** · **P13** · **P15** · **N02** | Easy-to-understand terrain/defense specialization. |

## Roster notes

### Global sharing

An Official Origin may appear in any number of Official AI preset pools. The Origin's name is a thematic identity for the build, **not a claim that the preset character said the line or belongs to the source work referenced by the Origin name**.

### Player availability

Official Origins are ordinary legal public builds and should be selectable by human players as curated ready-made Origins. Custom Origin creation remains available separately under the same canonical public catalogue.

### No forced category symmetry

The library does not need a fixed quota of naval, economic, nuclear, defensive, or other Origin categories. New Official Origins should be added when a coherent mechanical fantasy or AI-pool need exists, not to fill an arbitrary taxonomy.

### Intentional under-budget Origin

**O50 — Lucky Star** is intentionally a 5-point build. Its low spend is a deliberate roster/content choice for a geometry/flair-heavy Origin, not an authoring oversight. P54 mechanics are owned by the trait catalogue and the referenced spawn profile is owned by `STRATEGIC_SPAWN.md`.
