# Open Fufu — Origin Validation Coverage N01–N10

## Status and authority

This file is a continuation of [`../ORIGIN_VALIDATION_COVERAGE.md`](../ORIGIN_VALIDATION_COVERAGE.md) and uses the same canonical coverage model.

It owns **validation coverage/dependency tracing only** for N01–N10. Origin mechanics remain canonical in [`../ORIGIN_TRAIT_CATALOGUE.md`](../ORIGIN_TRAIT_CATALOGUE.md), and focused subsystem mechanics remain owned by their normal documents.

The relationship classes remain:

1. **Direct transformation** — the subsystem whose rule/value/permission/state the trait directly changes.
2. **Required integration seam** — trait-specific state/event flow crossing subsystem boundaries.
3. **External semantic dependency** — canonical state/mechanics the trait reads but does not own.
4. **Ordinary downstream consumption** — valid canonical state that ordinary consumers must already handle without redundant trait-specific implementations.

Negative traits additionally require explicit attention to **hard prohibitions, caps, admission rules, and precedence**. A positive modifier, discount, grant, or alternate route must not silently bypass a negative trait whose canonical effect is a hard legality constraint.

Mechanic-definition holes discovered here are blockers only. This file must not invent the missing gameplay rule.

# 1. N01–N10 coverage

## N01 — The Lost Decade

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Persistent-City contribution as consumed by Population Growth.
- Each completed City owned by the N01 holder contributes `20% less Population Growth` than the ordinary completed-level City contribution.
- The underlying City level remains unchanged; N01 transforms the City-derived growth contribution, not structure progression.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `owned active City completed level -> ordinary City growth contribution -> N01 reduction -> faction City-derived growth aggregate -> canonical Population Growth calculation`.
- The ordinary City table remains the pre-transform basis. For example, an ordinary L5 City contributes `+5%`; N01 must consume the effective reduced contribution rather than pretending the City became a different level.
- City-derived growth remains only one explicit growth input; terrain-share growth and the utilization curve continue through their ordinary hooks.
- Capturing or losing a City must add/remove the N01-adjusted City contribution according to current ownership.

**Explicit interactions**

- `N01 + P02`: P02 replaces the utilization curve while N01 changes the City-derived explicit growth contribution. Both hooks must survive composition; neither replaces the other.
- `N01 + P41`: a P41-purchased City becomes completed L5 when its construction lifecycle completes; N01 then reduces that ordinary L5 City growth contribution. N01 must not downgrade the structure level or change P41 purchase semantics.
- `N01 + P33`: P33's Train-stop Population grant is based on completed City level (`20 × level`), not the City's growth contribution. N01 must not reduce the P33 grant merely because both mechanics reference City level/state.
- Changes to City count/level from ordinary construction, capture, destruction, or other traits feed N01 through canonical structure state rather than bespoke pair formulas.

**Non-effects**

- N01 does not reduce Population Capacity, change City purchase/upgrade cost, alter Train-station eligibility, or modify the Population utilization curve.

## N02 — Flat Is Justice

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Terrain-conditioned land-combat offensive pressure.
- When an N02 holder attacks from a **Plains** source cell, that source's Plains offensive-pressure contribution is reduced by `25%` relative to the ordinary Plains source-pressure baseline.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `attacking source cell -> base terrain = Plains -> faction-effective N02 source-offense transform -> final local attacking pressure A -> canonical capture/settlement pressure calculation`.
- N02 must be evaluated from the actual attacking source cell for each engagement lane rather than from faction-wide Plains ownership share.
- Non-Plains source cells remain unaffected by N02.
- Terrain-conditioned source pressure must still compose with independent structure/Origin pressure sources through the canonical pressure-composition pipeline.

**Explicit interactions**

- P18, P19, P50, ordinary Command-Post support, and other offensive-pressure sources may coexist on a Plains engagement lane. N02 changes the Plains terrain component; representative multi-source pressure tests should prove the final composition without pair-testing every unrelated pressure trait.
- `N02 + P15` requires no same-cell special formula because P15 is Highland-specific while N02 is Plains-specific; one engagement source cell cannot simultaneously be both base terrains.
- Cross-faction defender effects such as P51/Fort defense remain ordinary opposing pressure inputs rather than N02-specific mechanics.

**Non-effects**

- N02 does not change Plains acquisition-speed multiplier, Plains Population-Growth terrain-share effect, terrain identity, movement, defensive pressure, or neutral settlement Population cost.

## N03 — I Hate Sand

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Terrain-conditioned land-combat defensive pressure.
- When an N03 holder defends a **Desert** target cell, that target's Desert defensive-pressure contribution is reduced by `33%` relative to the ordinary Desert target-defense baseline.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `target cell -> base terrain = Desert -> faction-effective N03 target-defense transform -> final local defending pressure D -> canonical capture calculation`.
- N03 changes the Desert terrain-defense component only; it does not remove an automatic defender or alter whether one exists.
- Fort/P51/other defensive support must continue to compose through their ordinary hooks when present on the same target.
- Non-Desert target cells remain unaffected.

**Explicit interactions**

- P51, ordinary Fort defense, P13-style terrain/Origin defense on other terrain, and other defensive modifiers should be exercised through representative multi-source defensive-pressure tests rather than an exhaustive pair matrix.
- `N03 + P14` does not require a special combat/economy formula merely because both reference Desert. P14 affects FFY events located on Desert; N03 affects defensive pressure on Desert target cells.

**Non-effects**

- N03 does not change Desert acquisition speed, Desert FFY terrain-share effect, terrain identity, movement, source offense, or structure legality.

## N04 — Northern Expedition

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Spatial FFY-event yield calculation.
- A positive FFY event whose canonical event location lies on **Mountain** receives `50% less FFY` for the N04 holder.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `qualifying positive FFY event -> canonical event location -> base terrain = Mountain -> N04 spatial yield percentage -> ordinary FFY modifier ordering`.
- N04 consumes the canonical event location supplied by the event owner. It must not invent locations for non-spatial passive sources merely to apply itself.
- The FFY owner already defines ordinary positive-event ordering: structural transformations, eligible yield percentages, same-axis combination, nonnegative clamp, then explicit hard-zero rules.
- Event families remain intact. N04 may affect Military/conquest, Naval/trade, Industrial, or other positive events only when that event actually has a canonical Mountain location and no focused rule excludes the spatial modifier.

**Explicit interactions**

- `N04 + P24`: a Mountain-located event inside a qualifying Fort area can receive both the Mountain penalty and Fort-area bonus through the ordinary same-axis yield composition pipeline.
- `N04 + N11`: if an event is both Mountain-located and inside a qualifying SAM area, N11's explicit hard-zero rule must win after ordinary yield percentages; N04 must not turn zero back into a nonzero result.
- `N04 + P14` has no same-location terrain overlap because one event location cannot have both Mountain and Desert as its base terrain.
- P05 and any other event-producing trait must only interact with N04 once that event's canonical location is defined. P05's already-recorded location blocker therefore also blocks exact P05+N04 expectations.

**Non-effects**

- N04 does not modify FFY purchase prices, losses, Transport embarkation costs, passive non-spatial FFY sources, or Mountain combat/terrain properties.

## N05 — Curse of the Abyss

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Hard territorial-acquisition target legality.
- Fallout terrain is **uncapturable** for the N05 holder.
- This is a prohibition on acquiring a Fallout target, not a pressure/capture-speed modifier.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `target cell Fallout overlay + N05 holder -> acquisition legality = forbidden` before capture/settlement progress can successfully transfer ownership.
- The prohibition must apply wherever the ordinary territorial-acquisition pipeline is used, including hostile land capture, neutral settlement, and amphibious land engagement when the target remains Fallout.
- The underlying base terrain retains its ordinary identity; N05 keys from the Fallout overlay rather than replacing terrain classification.
- Controller/mechanics legality surfaces must agree with authoritative resolution so controllers cannot receive a legal quote/path to an acquisition that runtime later treats differently.

**Explicit interactions**

- `N05 + P16`: N05 wins as the hard prohibition. P16 removes ordinary Fallout acquisition resistance but does not override `cannot capture Fallout`.
- `N05 + P35`: the holder may deliberately create Fallout that it subsequently cannot reacquire while N05 applies.
- `N05 + P44`: the holder may create radioactive/neutralized Fallout through P44 and still be unable to acquire those cells.
- `N05 + N18`: N18's non-Fallout progress penalty is irrelevant to Fallout targets; N05 forbids those targets outright.
- Cross-faction P35/P44-created Fallout is consumed through the same ordinary Fallout-overlay legality test; no special creator identity matters.

**Non-effects**

- N05 does not alter Fallout movement, structure-buildability inherited from base terrain, source/target pressure, Fallout persistence/removal after some other faction's successful capture, or non-Fallout acquisition.

## N06 — No Second Season

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Persistent-structure **upgrade transaction** legality.
- The holder cannot spend FFY to upgrade buildings/structures.
- Ordinary structure construction/purchase and ownership remain separate actions unless another trait changes them.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `structure upgrade request -> canonical effective upgrade FFY cost -> N06 legality gate -> authoritative atomic decision validation`.
- Ordinary paid L2–L5 upgrade attempts must be rejected for the N06 holder rather than merely accepted and charged zero by accident.
- Existing completed levels remain active; N06 does not downgrade structures.
- Capturing/granting/otherwise legally obtaining a higher-level structure does not itself constitute spending FFY on an upgrade.

**Explicit interactions**

- `N06 + P41`: explicitly legal. P41 creates a purchased City directly at L5 as one build/purchase transaction; N06 must not reinterpret that as four forbidden FFY upgrade actions.
- `N06 + P17`: P17 discounts upgrade FFY cost, but an ordinary upgrade still remains an FFY upgrade transaction; a mere discount must not bypass N06.
- `N06 + P21`: P21 applies to first **purchases** of structure types, not upgrades, so it does not create an N06 upgrade exception.
- Structure grants such as P20/P37 remain grants and do not violate N06 merely because the resulting structure has a completed level.

**Non-effects**

- N06 does not forbid ordinary L1 structure purchases, structure capture, structure use, or non-FFY effects merely because a structure could otherwise be upgraded later.

**Boundary note**

- The current catalogue contains no trait that explicitly makes an ordinary upgrade transaction cost exactly `0 FFY`. If a future mechanic does so, the wording `cannot spend FFY to upgrade` may require a canonical decision about whether a zero-FFY upgrade is legal. No current certification case needs that hypothetical rule to be invented in #31.

## N07 — One Piece

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Persistent-structure ownership/admission legality.
- The holder may own **at most one persistent structure of each canonical structure type**.
- This is a hard ownership cap, not merely a purchase limit.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `structure acquisition request -> owned physical structures + committed non-materialized reservations + current-proposal reservations -> N07 cap`.
- A materialized under-construction structure is already an owned physical structure and must not also be double-counted as a separate pending reservation.
- Atomic same-decision validation and concurrent construction admission must not allow two build commands/production paths to reserve the same final ownership slot.
- The cap is checked at every acquisition path that can make a persistent structure belong to the holder, including:
  - controller purchase/build;
  - enemy-structure capture/ownership transfer;
  - starting grants such as P20;
  - successful-landing grants such as P37;
  - future scenario/start-state structure grants.
- Destroying/losing the currently owned structure releases the ownership slot at the authoritative ownership transition.
- Structure upgrades do not create another structure and therefore do not consume an additional same-type slot.
- Same-tick capture resolution releases outgoing slots before evaluating incoming transfers and uses stable cell/structure ordering when several incoming structures compete for a smaller number of slots.

**Explicit interactions**

- `N07 + P11`: P11's unlocked SAM entitlement and N07's one-per-type rule are both mandatory ownership constraints; an acquisition must satisfy both. P11's zero FFY cost does not bypass N07, and normalized cap composition remains #43-owned.
- `N07 + P20`: P20's starting Silo is a grant and must pass the one-Silo ownership cap after #32 supplies the start-state placement/order. A successful grant is immediately active L1; a rejected grant creates nothing.
- `N07 + P21`: free first purchase still requires ordinary legality, so P21 cannot consume a zero-FFY purchase through a second same-type structure that N07 forbids.
- `N07 + P37`: the exact-cell landing Fort grant is skipped when the holder's Fort slot is already occupied; the successful landing/cell capture remains successful. If N07 instead rejects a captured non-Fort structure on that landing cell, its destruction may free the cell before the later Fort grant is independently evaluated.
- `N07 + P34`: when a captured Factory would overflow the holder's one-Factory cap, the territorial capture succeeds but the incoming Factory is destroyed instead of transferred. Because no successful transfer occurs, P34 conquest provenance does not arise.
- `N07 + P46`: expanded terrain eligibility does not expand ownership capacity.

**Resolved mechanic-definition result (#45)**

- The generic structure owner now closes the former blocker: an inadmissible `CAPTURE_TRANSFER` is resolved as `DESTROYED_ON_CAPTURE` while political cell capture remains successful; an inadmissible grant simply creates no structure and never rolls back its triggering event.
- Build-only restrictions/terrain legality are not re-applied to existing structures on `CAPTURE_TRANSFER`, while hard ownership caps remain universal across purchase, grant, and transfer paths.

## N08 — It's Just Decoration

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Persistent Fort effective combat profile.
- Forts owned by the N08 holder provide **no defensive-pressure bonus**.
- Fort structure identity, completed level, coverage field, cost, and ownership remain unless another rule changes them.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `owned active Fort -> effective defensive-pressure magnitude = 0 -> land-combat defensive-pressure composition`.
- A Fort still does not create an automatic defender; N08 simply removes the Fort contribution when an ordinary defender exists.
- Fort coverage must remain available to mechanics that explicitly consume the **area** independently of defensive-pressure magnitude.

**Explicit interactions**

- `N08 + P03`: P03 becomes inert against the N08 holder's Fort defensive contribution because there is no Fort defensive bonus to ignore.
- `N08 + P09`: N08 must win on the defensive-pressure axis; P09 must not restore a positive Fort defensive bonus through its `+9%` component. P09 cost and coverage transformations remain independently applicable.
- `N08 + P18`: P18 qualifies attacking source cells from Fort area; N08 does **not** disable that area-based offensive mechanic.
- `N08 + P24`: Fort-area FFY qualification remains because coverage persists.
- `N08 + P50`: P50's offensive magnitude is defined from the Fort's **effective defensive-pressure magnitude**. Under N08 that magnitude is zero, so P50 must not resurrect a hidden baseline defensive value as offense.
- `N08 + P51`: P51 Command-Post defensive pressure remains independently meaningful; N08 only zeroes Fort-derived defense.
- `N08 + N10`: N10 can still contract Fort coverage even though the Fort's defensive magnitude is zero because other area consumers may still care about the field.

**Non-effects**

- N08 does not remove Fort coverage, ownership, construction, P18/P24 area qualification, or other non-defensive Fort functions.

## N09 — Medieval Isekai

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Persistent-structure **Factory build admission**.
- The holder cannot build/purchase new Factories.
- This is a hard structure-type construction prohibition, not a rule that Factories cannot ever be owned or used.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `structure build request type = FACTORY + N09 holder -> illegal` before affordability/payment or construction begins.
- P21 or another purchase-cost transformation must not bypass the Factory build prohibition merely by reducing FFY consumption.
- Terrain permission changes such as P46 do not bypass the type prohibition.
- If the holder legally acquires a Factory through another canonical path, that existing Factory remains an ordinary persistent structure and its Train/Tank/repair services may operate unless another rule forbids them.
- `CAPTURE_TRANSFER` preserves the existing physical Factory and does not re-run N09's build-only prohibition or the new owner's terrain build eligibility.

**Explicit interactions**

- `N09 + P07`: explicitly awkward but legal. P07 remains active on any legally acquired Factory even though N09 prevents building one.
- `N09 + P34`: explicitly legal and strategically coherent: conquered Factories can provide P34's transformed effect while built Factories remain forbidden.
- `N09 + P43`: Heavy Artillery production can matter if a Factory is acquired through a legal non-build path; N09 must not suppress the P43 chassis transformation merely because it prevented Factory construction.
- `N09 + P46`: P46 removes Tundra terrain prohibition but N09 still forbids Factory construction there or anywhere else.
- `N09 + P21`: first-Factory free-purchase entitlement cannot override a hard Factory build prohibition.
- `N09 + N17`: both may combine into an intentionally harsh/inert build where Factories cannot be built and captured enemy Factories are destroyed instead of transferred; this remains legal rather than triggering a hidden compatibility rule.

**Non-effects**

- N09 does not by itself destroy owned Factories, disable captured/granted Factories, forbid Factory upgrades, or suppress Train/Tank/repair behavior from a Factory the holder legally owns.

## N10 — Domain Contraction

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Persistent Fort effective spatial profile.
- Reduces Fort **coverage area** by `25%` relative to the ordinary/effective pre-N10 Fort area.
- N10 changes where Fort-area mechanics apply; it does not by itself change Fort defensive-pressure magnitude.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `Fort completed level -> ordinary/effective Fort coverage -> N10 area transform -> deterministic raster coverage -> every mechanic consuming Fort area`.
- Land combat must use the same effective field as controller/mechanics projections; there must not be one radius for Fort defense and a different stale radius for Origin interactions.
- Same-type overlapping Forts continue to use the canonical strongest-applicable same-type field behavior after each Fort's effective area is resolved.

**Explicit interactions**

- `N10 + P09`: both change Fort coverage **area** on the same axis. The final effective area needs one canonical composition rule; cost/defensive-pressure components of P09 remain separate.
- `N10 + P03`: smaller coverage changes where any Fort defensive contribution exists for P03 to ignore.
- `N10 + P18`: directly contracts the self/fixed-teammate Fort area that can qualify an attacking source cell for P18.
- `N10 + P24`: contracts the area in which located FFY events can receive P24's Fort-area bonus.
- `N10 + P50`: contracts P50's Fort-derived offensive support region.
- `N10 + P51`: changes where a Fort defensive field can overlap P51 Command-Post defense and therefore where cross-type complement composition is needed.
- `N10 + N08`: Fort area remains mechanically relevant to area consumers even when N08 sets Fort defensive magnitude to zero.
- A P37-granted Fort uses the same effective N10 coverage after the grant exists; grant origin does not create a separate Fort geometry implementation.

**Blockers / mechanic-definition findings**

- The ordinary structure registry stores Fort **coverage radius**, while N10 authors a `25% reduced coverage area`. As already identified for P09, deterministic conversion from authored area modifiers to raster coverage/radius must be canonical rather than guessed.
- `P09 + N10` places `+10% area` and `-25% area` on the same Fort-coverage axis. The inspected canonical material does not state whether those percentages combine additively before geometry conversion, multiplicatively, or through another explicit rule. Exact combined coverage therefore remains uncertifiable until the same-axis composition rule is closed.

# 3. Validation-boundary findings from N01–N10

The first negative batch strengthens several boundaries from the positive audit:

- **Action admission / legality is a first-class validation boundary.** N05, N06, N07, and N09 cannot be modeled as ordinary numeric modifiers after an action has already been accepted.
- **Hard prohibitions/caps require explicit precedence.** Discounts, free purchases, terrain permissions, and grants do not bypass a prohibition merely because they operate on a nearby axis.
- **Ownership caps cover all acquisition paths.** Build, capture, start-state grants, amphibious grants, and future scenario grants converge on the canonical structure-admission contract; capture-transfer rejection destroys the incoming structure without undoing territorial capture, while grant rejection creates nothing.
- **Effective structure geometry is shared state.** N10 confirms that Fort coverage must be resolved once and consumed consistently by defense, P18, P24, P50, P03 qualification, APIs, and overlap logic.
- **Spatial FFY modifiers consume event-owner location state.** N04 should not duplicate event-location logic inside Origin code.
- **Negative numeric transforms remain domain projections rather than bespoke systems.** N01/N02/N03 fit cleanly into growth/pressure projections, while N08 fits the effective Fort profile.

These findings support retaining an explicit **admission/legality layer inside the relevant gameplay domains** rather than creating a separate monolithic Origin validator for prohibitions.
