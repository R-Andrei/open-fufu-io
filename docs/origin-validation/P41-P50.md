# Open Fufu — Origin Validation Coverage P41–P50

## Status and authority

This file is a continuation of [`../ORIGIN_VALIDATION_COVERAGE.md`](../ORIGIN_VALIDATION_COVERAGE.md) and uses the same canonical coverage model.

It owns **validation coverage/dependency tracing only** for P41–P50. Origin mechanics remain canonical in [`../ORIGIN_TRAIT_CATALOGUE.md`](../ORIGIN_TRAIT_CATALOGUE.md), and focused subsystem mechanics remain owned by their normal documents.

The relationship classes remain:

1. **Direct transformation** — the subsystem whose rule/value/permission/state the trait directly changes.
2. **Required integration seam** — trait-specific state/event flow crossing subsystem boundaries.
3. **External semantic dependency** — canonical state/mechanics the trait reads but does not own.
4. **Ordinary downstream consumption** — valid canonical state that ordinary consumers must already handle without redundant trait-specific implementations.

This batch additionally distinguishes **cross-faction Origin interactions** where one faction's trait changes state that another faction's trait explicitly queries. Those are real validation obligations even though both traits cannot be selected by one Origin.

Mechanic-definition holes discovered here are blockers only. This file must not invent the missing gameplay rule.

# 1. P41–P50 coverage

## P41 — Level 5

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Persistent City purchase transaction.
- A purchased City is created by one direct **level-5 purchase** action rather than one L1 purchase followed by four upgrade actions.
- Effective purchase price is `95%` of the ordinary cumulative L1→L5 City cost.
- With the current ordinary cumulative City cost of `2,100,000 FFY`, the P41 purchase affordability/spend basis is `1,995,000 FFY` before any other explicit purchase-transaction transformation.
- The direct-L5 purchase uses exactly **one ordinary City construction interval: 5 seconds**.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `City build quote -> P41 resultingLevel = 5 -> buildTicks = 5 seconds worth of simulation ticks -> P41 effective purchase price -> ordinary affordability/payment -> one construction lifecycle -> active completed L5 City`.
- The action must not emit or internally execute four upgrade transactions or four upgrade timers.
- From successful transaction commit through the five-second construction interval the City is one owned physical structure with `completedLevel = none`, `active = false`, and `construction.targetLevel = 5`; `construction.remainingTicks` counts down deterministically. It already occupies any applicable ownership slot.
- On completion the same physical City atomically sets `completedLevel = 5`, clears its in-progress construction state, and becomes active. No L1–L4 completed state is emitted along the way.
- If the City is captured while still constructing, canonical structure capture preserves the same pending target level and remaining construction time under the new owner rather than completing, restarting, or downgrading it.
- Once active, the City contributes the ordinary L5 City effects: `+5%` City-derived Population Growth and ordinary Train-station eligibility. P41 does not create alternate City effect formulas.
- Captured already-completed lower-level Cities remain at the captured level. P41 does not retroactively promote them to L5.

**Explicit interactions**

- `P41 + P21`: P41 supplies the canonical effective first-City purchase requirement of `1,995,000 FFY`. If this is the first successful City purchase, P21 requires that affordability/legality first, then makes `ffySpent = 0` and advances its first-City entitlement exactly once. Failed admission consumes neither FFY nor the entitlement.
- `P41 + N06`: explicitly legal because P41 is one purchase rather than FFY upgrade spending. N06 therefore does not forbid the initial direct-L5 purchase.
- `P41 + P17`: P17 is an upgrade-transaction multiplier. P41 does not secretly execute upgrade transactions, so P17 must not be applied four times to the direct-L5 action. Any generic purchase-price modifier would have to act on the final P41 purchase hook explicitly.
- `P41 + P33`: an active P41 City is completed level 5, so each qualifying P33 Train event at that City grants `20 × 5 = 100` Available Population while Capacity permits. An inactive in-progress P41 City has no completed level and cannot provide that completed-L5 station effect early.
- `P41 + N01`: the City supplies its ordinary L5 City-derived growth contribution only after completion; N01 later modifies that City-growth axis rather than changing P41's created level.
- `P41 + P46`: if the target cell is owned Tundra and all other ordinary structure-placement rules are satisfied, P46 may make that terrain eligible for the P41 City purchase. P41 does not itself bypass terrain legality.
- `P41 + N07`: the direct-L5 City is still one physical City; successful admission reserves/occupies the one City slot immediately and construction cannot be used to oversubscribe the cap.

**Non-effects**

- P41 does not alter captured completed-City level, City Population Capacity, Train routing, or ordinary City effects beyond causing a purchased City to complete directly at level 5.

**Resolved mechanic-definition result (#45)**

- The former construction-time blocker is closed: `StructureBuildQuote.buildTicks` for a P41 City is one ordinary City construction interval, currently 5 seconds, with explicit fresh-construction state followed by atomic activation at completed L5.

## P42 — The Price of Empire

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Warship purchase transaction:
  - `0 FFY` required/spent for the Warship purchase itself;
  - exactly `2,000 Available Population` permanently consumed per successful purchase;
  - only Available Population may pay this cost.
- Naval Warship effective combat profile: `-33% attack range`.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `Available Population -> unit-build affordability/atomic decision validation -> exactly 2,000 Population spent -> ordinary Warship construction`.
- A faction with fewer than 2,000 Available Population cannot fund the purchase even if Total Population is larger because some Population is committed/aboard.
- The 2,000 Population is removed from Total Population permanently; it is not stored as crew, does not return when the Warship dies, and does not change Population Capacity.
- Atomic multi-command validation must not allow several simultaneous P42 Warship purchases to spend the same Available Population.
- `effective Warship attack range -> autonomous naval gun target acquisition/fire legality` must consume the P42 range transformation.

**Explicit interactions**

- `P42 + P23`: explicitly legal. P23 changes Warship range/damage/speed and enforces one-Warship ownership; P42 changes purchase resource and the same attack-range axis. The hard P23 cap remains a normal admission constraint even though P42 uses Population instead of FFY. Under the canonical unit-admission contract an admitted under-construction Warship already occupies the P23 slot and an atomic proposal cannot reserve a second slot.
- `P42 + P29`: explicitly legal. P42's Warship **attack-range** penalty must not shrink or otherwise redefine P29 strategic-weapon launcher behavior; strategic launch legality/range/origin remains P29/strategic-weapon state.
- `P42 + P30`: explicitly legal. P30 removes naval gunfire against ships, so P42's naval attack-range penalty may become inert for that forbidden attack while Population purchase cost and other Warship lifecycle rules remain active. Trade Ship capture distance is not an attack-range stat and must remain ordinary unless explicitly modified.
- `P42 + P22`: rank progression/max-health/damage remain ordinary; P42 does not modify Naval XP or rank.
- `P42 + N12`: explicitly awkward but legal. N12's hard build prohibition prevents Warship purchase; P42 does not turn Population into a bypass for a forbidden unit.
- `P42 + P31`: Port repair consumes the resulting Warship's ordinary health state; P42 does not create recoverable crew or a special repair resource.

**Non-effects**

- P42 does not change Warship construction time, health, movement speed, shell damage, capture radius, rank lifecycle, or strategic-launcher behavior except where another trait independently modifies those systems.

**Blocker / mechanic-definition finding**

- `P23 + P42` places `+20% range` and `-33% attack range` on the same Warship range axis, but the inspected canonical material does not yet state the exact same-axis Origin composition rule for these two modifiers. Validation needs one deterministic effective range rather than choosing additive-vs-multiplicative composition by convention.

## P43 — The Devil of the Rhine

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Complete transformation of every Tank chassis owned/built by the holder into **Heavy Artillery**.
- Factory Tank construction produces the transformed chassis while retaining the ordinary active-Tank-chassis cost curve as the pre-transform cost basis.
- Canonical transformed profile:
  - build time `10s`;
  - purchase price `1.50×` ordinary current Tank price;
  - final movement `0.50×` ordinary final Tank movement;
  - anti-armor/Population attack range `1.50×` ordinary Tank range;
  - max health `1,000`;
  - anti-armor attack `1,000 damage / 12s`;
  - Population attack `1,000 Population / 12s`;
  - Train interception disabled;
  - same chassis terrain barriers as Tank;
  - projectiles may cross terrain the chassis itself cannot traverse.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `Factory unit-build quote -> requested TANK -> resulting HEAVY_ARTILLERY -> ordinary active-chassis count curve -> 1.50× purchase price -> 10s construction`.
- Heavy Artillery must continue counting against the same active Tank-chassis purchase curve; changing the public unit type must not reset/escape progressive Tank pricing.
- `ordinary terrain-specific final Tank speed -> P43 0.50× final movement`: the movement transform applies after the ordinary terrain movement result, while the chassis keeps the same traversal barriers.
- `ordinary Tank range -> 1.50× Heavy-Artillery attack range` for anti-armor and Population attacks.
- Anti-armor attacks remain autonomous and do not require/create `atWar`; Population attacks inherit the ordinary Tank requirement that the owner/target sides currently be `atWar`.
- Train interception must be absent from the transformed attack set rather than merely reduced to zero damage.
- `Factory repair -> Heavy Artillery HP`: ordinary Tank repair-retreat threshold/Factory repair semantics continue to consume the transformed chassis unless another explicit rule changes them.
- Projectile path legality must be distinct from chassis path legality: a Heavy-Artillery projectile may cross Mountain/Shallow Water/other chassis-blocked terrain even though the unit may not path through it.

**Explicit interactions**

- `P43 + P44`: explicitly canonical. P44 uses Heavy Artillery's larger radioactive footprint/cap (`radius 5`, up to `50` eligible cells) after successful Heavy-Artillery Population attacks.
- `P43 + P34`: if/when P34's doubled-Factory effect is closed over Tank production/repair hooks, Heavy Artillery must consume those same Factory hooks rather than receive a separate P34 implementation.
- `P43 + N09`: N09 may prevent building Factories, but P43 remains applicable to Tank chassis obtained through any later canonical legal Factory access/acquisition path.
- P45/P49 or ordinary observation may determine whether an autonomous target is legally observed; P43 does not create an observation bypass.

**Non-effects**

- P43 does not create a second health bonus, generic structure damage, territory capture, Train interception, or new terrain traversal permissions for the chassis.

## P44 — Nobel Prize

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Trigger: a successful Tank-chassis **Population attack** by the P44 holder.
- Output: deterministic territorial neutralization plus Fallout application to eligible nearby enemy-owned population-bearing cells.
- Baseline Tank footprint: at most `10` eligible cells in Manhattan radius `2`.
- P43 Heavy-Artillery footprint: at most `50` eligible cells in Manhattan radius `5`.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `successful Population attack -> current target point -> collect current enemy-owned population-bearing cells -> exclude structure-occupied cells -> deterministic distance/CellId order -> neutralize selected cells -> apply Fallout`.
- Anti-armor combat and Train interception must never trigger P44.
- The ordinary direct Population damage resolves as the Tank/Heavy-Artillery attack effect; P44 adds no second direct Population-damage multiplier.
- Neutralized cells cease to be owned by the victim and therefore cease contributing that victim's Capacity according to their effective population-bearing classification. P44 does not transfer those cells to the attacker.
- P44 neutralization is not ordinary territorial **capture**: it must not fire P05/P34/P47/N17 or ordinary structure-capture logic merely because political ownership becomes neutral.
- Structure-occupied candidate cells are excluded before deterministic ordering; the resolver must not expand beyond the authored Manhattan radius merely to fill the cap.
- Overlapping/repeated attacks must remain deterministic and may only neutralize cells that are still eligible/currently enemy-owned at their canonical resolution point.

**Explicit interactions**

- `P43 + P44`: explicitly legal radioactive Heavy Artillery; use the Heavy-Artillery radius/cap and the same deterministic candidate ordering.
- `P44 + P16`: P44 creates Fallout; P16 may later remove ordinary Fallout acquisition resistance for the same holder when that faction subsequently tries to acquire otherwise legal Fallout.
- `P44 + N05`: awkward but legal. The holder can create Fallout it later cannot capture while N05 applies.
- `P44 + P35`: both produce ordinary Fallout through different triggers. The shared overlay mechanics should be validated once while preserving the distinct abandonment-vs-Population-attack trigger paths.
- `P44 attacker -> P48 defender` is an explicit **cross-faction Origin interaction**. P48 makes the defender's owned Shallow Water population-bearing. Because P44 selects **enemy-owned population-bearing cells**, defender-owned P48 Shallow Water inside the authored footprint is eligible unless another P44 exclusion applies. P48 does not need to be present on the attacker.
- Negative cross-system assertion: P44 neutralization of Marsh is not a successful enemy capture and therefore must not trigger P47.

**Non-effects**

- P44 does not alter Tank chassis movement, direct attack damage, structure damage, territorial capture speed, or Fallout capture legality.

## P45 — Hidden Leaf Village

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Tactical observation/visibility filtering based on **Forest owned by the P45 holder**.
- Enemy tactical observation cannot reveal the holder's units, persistent structures, or manifested operational state in concealed Forest interior.
- Forest terrain identity and political ownership remain public.
- Exposed Forest-front boundary cells remain ordinarily observable.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `base terrain + political ownership -> P45 concealed-cell classification -> lawful observation projection -> controller/AI visible units/structures/operations`.
- The concealment filter must apply after determining ordinary observation coverage: an enemy Observation Post or other ordinary legal observation source must not reveal protected P45 contents merely because its reveal radius reaches the concealed interior.
- The P45 holder continues to observe its own state normally; the rule is enemy tactical concealment, not self-blindness.
- Ownership changes must update concealment with the current P45 holder rather than permanently tagging Forest cells.
- A direct hostile manifestation from concealment exposes only the canonical minimum state needed to identify/respond to that manifestation; it must not reveal unrelated nearby concealed contents.
- Terrain type and political owner remain visible even when contained tactical state is hidden.

**Explicit interactions**

- `P45 + P49`: both may conceal the holder's state through different spatial predicates. Overlap should behave as the union of applicable concealment, not multiply a numeric concealment strength or allow one concealment source to cancel the other.
- Ordinary Observation Posts remain useful outside P45 concealment and for state belonging to factions not protected by P45.
- P43/Tank/Warship/operation target legality consumes whatever tactical state is lawfully observable; P45 does not give those combat systems bespoke hidden-state access.

**Non-effects**

- P45 does not hide Forest terrain, territorial ownership, controller memory, unmanifested plans, or state outside the canonical tactical observation model.

**Blocker / mechanic-definition finding**

- Executable certification needs an exact canonical definition of **Forest interior vs exposed Forest-front boundary cells** and of the **minimum directly relevant state** revealed by a hostile manifestation. The current prose gives the intended information boundary but not enough deterministic geometry/payload semantics to assert exact controller-visible results across arbitrary Forest shapes.

## P46 — Northern Lands

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Faction-effective terrain/structure-build eligibility.
- Owned Tundra becomes eligible for ordinary persistent-structure construction for the P46 holder.
- P46 changes no other Tundra property.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `owned Tundra + P46 -> terrainSpec persistentStructureTerrainEligible -> ordinary structure-build quote/placement checks -> ordinary construction lifecycle`.
- Every structure type still obeys its own non-terrain legality: ownership caps, P11 SAM slots, Port coast/water-interface requirement, structure-type prohibitions, affordability, etc.
- A structure legally created on Tundra remains an ordinary persistent structure after completion.
- If that structure is later captured by a faction without P46, the new owner may own/use the existing structure normally; lack of P46 only prevents **new** Tundra construction and does not retroactively destroy/inactivate an existing legal structure.

**Explicit interactions**

- `P46 + P41`: P46 may make owned Tundra terrain-eligible for the direct-L5 City purchase; P41 still owns level/price and all other placement legality remains ordinary.
- `P46 + N09`: P46 removes the Tundra terrain prohibition but N09 still forbids Factory construction. Terrain permission must not override a structure-type prohibition.
- `P46 + N07`: P46 does not bypass one-of-each structure ownership caps.
- `P46 + P11`: a SAM on Tundra still requires a P11-unlocked SAM slot where P11 applies.
- `P46 + P21`: the free-first-purchase transaction can only commit after P46 and all other ordinary placement/legality checks succeed.

**Non-effects**

- Tundra remains `0 Capacity`, non-population-bearing, non-spawn-eligible, 80% acquisition speed, +5% target defense, and otherwise ordinary terrain. P46 does not make Tanks traverse new terrain or change terrain-share effects.

## P47 — This Is Poison

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Successful hostile cell-capture casualty resolution.
- Trigger is an enemy successfully capturing a **Marsh** cell that belonged to the P47 holder immediately before capture.
- After ordinary capture resolution, the capturing faction loses one additional Population.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `pre-capture owner/terrain snapshot -> ordinary successful capture -> ordinary ownership/casualty resolution -> P47 post-capture Population loss on winning faction`.
- P47 must trigger even if the Marsh cell had no automatic defender; its extra casualty is independent of baseline automatic-defender casualty eligibility.
- If the cell was automatically defended, the attacker first receives the ordinary successful-capture consequence and P47 then adds its separate +1 Population loss.
- Failed capture/progress ticks, neutral settlement, deliberate abandonment, P44 neutralization, and other non-capture ownership changes must not trigger P47.
- Amphibious land combat or Minor-Faction land combat that reaches the same ordinary successful-capture event must use the same P47 rule; there is no separate Marsh-capture implementation for those actors.

**Explicit interactions**

- `P47 + P38`: same defending Origin may carry both. On successful capture of an automatically defended Marsh cell, P38 preserves the defender while the attacker's ordinary capture casualty still occurs and P47 adds its independent extra attacker casualty. Neither trait should erase the other's axis.
- `P47 + P35`: deliberate P35 relinquishment is not enemy capture and must not trigger P47 merely because a Marsh cell becomes neutral.
- `P47 defender <- N18 attacker` is ordinary cross-faction composition: N18 may slow the attacker's non-Fallout acquisition progress, but once capture succeeds P47's post-capture casualty still applies.
- Negative assertion: P44 neutralization is not capture and does not trigger P47.

**Non-effects**

- P47 does not alter Marsh acquisition speed, source offense, target defense, ownership transfer, or automatic-defender existence.

**Blocker / mechanic-definition finding**

- `the capturing faction loses +1 Population` does not yet identify the exact debit source/state. Validation needs to know whether the casualty is removed from the winning offensive commitment, Available Population, or another canonical faction-level Population pool, including deterministic behavior when the chosen source has no spare Population after ordinary capture casualties. The validation layer must not select that accounting rule.

## P48 — Aqua's Blessing

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Faction-effective base-terrain classification.
- **Owned** Shallow Water is population-bearing for the P48 holder and contributes exactly `+1 Population Capacity/cell`.
- Every mechanic that queries population-bearing classification must consume the owner-effective classification rather than hard-code Shallow Water as globally `0 Capacity`.
- All other Shallow-Water properties remain unchanged.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `political ownership + owner Origin -> effective Shallow-Water populationBearing/Capacity -> faction Population Capacity aggregate`.
- Acquiring Shallow Water as a P48 holder adds Capacity; losing it removes that Capacity. If the new owner also has P48, Capacity transfers to the new owner; otherwise the captured Shallow Water reverts to ordinary non-population-bearing state for that owner.
- Population growth and other ordinary Capacity consumers then consume the resulting Capacity normally rather than implementing P48-specific formulas.
- `terrainShare(T)` uses all owned population-bearing cells as its denominator. P48-owned Shallow Water must therefore enter that denominator even though Shallow Water has no own terrain-share bonus, changing effective Plains/Desert share percentages through the canonical generic classification query.
- Controller/mechanics projections must expose the faction-effective classification consistently wherever `populationBearing` or `terrainSpec(..., factionId)` is queried.

**Spawn / initialization seam**

- P48 does **not** change Shallow Water's ordinary exact-origin/spawn-seed eligibility; an exact origin may not become Shallow Water merely because the faction has P48.
- The starting-footprint resolver may ordinarily claim ownable Shallow Water while growing/traversing. Its quota rule says population-bearing claimed cells consume Initial-Territory quota. Once a candidate is claimed for a P48 faction it is owner-effectively population-bearing, so Spawn validation must use the resulting owner-effective classification for quota accounting rather than permanently treating every Shallow-Water claim as free `0 Capacity` territory.
- This closes the earlier P01/P39 inspection point: P01's modified total and P39's split quotas must be filled according to the same owner-effective population-bearing accounting while preserving their own quota/Population rules.

**Neutral-settlement seam**

- Neutral Shallow Water is not yet **owned by the P48 holder**, so it retains the ordinary non-population-bearing classification during the neutral settlement-cost calculation and costs `0 Population` under the baseline rule. After successful acquisition, P48 makes the resulting owned cell population-bearing and adds Capacity.
- `P48 + P36` therefore must not manufacture a `0.5` Population cost for neutral Shallow Water; P36 modifies qualifying population-bearing neutral settlement cost, while the target is still ordinary non-population-bearing Shallow Water at that point.

**Explicit interactions**

- `P48 + P01/P39`: Spawn quota accounting uses P48 owner-effective classification for any Shallow Water actually claimed by the footprint while P01/P39 continue to own total/split quota rules.
- `P48 + P02`: P48 changes Capacity; P02 consumes Capacity/utilization through ordinary Population Growth. This is primarily canonical state propagation rather than a second P02 implementation, but generated projection/property coverage should include P48-derived Capacity states.
- `P44 attacker -> P48 defender`: explicit cross-faction interaction. Defender-owned P48 Shallow Water is population-bearing and therefore may enter P44's eligible enemy-cell set.
- P52 will need explicit review because it directly computes economy from `Capacity - Total Population`.

**Non-effects**

- P48 does not make Shallow Water structure-buildable, change land/naval traversal, change its 70% acquisition speed, alter source/target pressure, or make it a legal exact spawn seed.

## P49 — Laughing Man

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Persistent Observation Post effective observation profile.
- Owned active Observation Posts of the P49 holder lose their ordinary tactical **REVEAL** function.
- Instead, each Post's ordinary completed-level observation radius becomes an **ENEMY_BLACKOUT** field concealing the P49 holder's units, persistent structures, and manifested operational state from enemies inside that area.
- The Observation Post itself and the existence/extent of the blackout field remain public.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `Observation Post owner/level -> ordinary observation radius -> P49 ENEMY_BLACKOUT field -> lawful controller/AI observation projection`.
- The holder must not receive ordinary Observation-Post reveal from its P49 Posts; other independent legal observation sources remain ordinary.
- Enemy ordinary reveal coverage overlapping the blackout must not reveal the P49 holder's protected contents merely because a hostile Observation Post also covers the cell.
- The blackout is faction-specific: it conceals the P49 holder's state, not arbitrary third-party units/structures that happen to occupy the same area.
- Same-holder P49 Post overlap forms the **union** of blackout coverage and does not increase concealment strength.
- On structure ownership transfer, the effective Observation-Post profile follows the current owner's Origin. A captured P49 Post in the hands of a non-P49 faction becomes an ordinary Observation Post; a P49 owner transforms a legally acquired Post through the same effective profile.
- A hostile manifestation from the blackout reveals only the canonical minimum state required for response, without exposing unrelated concealed contents.

**Explicit interactions**

- `P49 + P45`: two independent concealment predicates may cover the same P49-holder state. Their overlap is union/continued concealment, not numerical stacking or cancellation.
- `P49 + P46`: P46 may permit construction of the Observation Post on owned Tundra; P49 then transforms its observation effect normally.
- `P49 + N17`: if N17 prevents Observation-Post transfer by destroying the structure, the receiving faction never obtains a Post whose observation profile could be transformed.
- Other structure-purchase traits change how/where the Post is obtained but not P49's reveal-to-blackout transformation.

**Non-effects**

- P49 does not hide the Observation Post itself, blackout-field existence/extent, terrain, political ownership, controller memory, or unmanifested plans. It does not globally blind enemies to unrelated factions.

**Blocker / mechanic-definition finding**

- P49 shares P45's unresolved **minimum hostile-manifestation reveal** requirement. Exact certification needs a canonical visibility precedence/payload contract describing what protected state is surfaced when concealed activity directly manifests hostility and how that temporary/necessary reveal is represented through controller-visible APIs. The broad `ENEMY_BLACKOUT` effect is surfaced, but the exception boundary is not yet executable enough for exact conformance tests.

## P50 — Iserlohn Fortress

**Direct transformations / owners**

- Persistent Fort effective support field as consumed by land-combat offensive-pressure composition.
- Every **owned active Fort** additionally projects offensive pressure equal to that Fort's effective defensive-pressure magnitude across the Fort's existing effective coverage.

**Required seams / dependencies**

- `owned active Fort completed level -> effective Fort defensive-pressure magnitude + effective Fort coverage -> P50 offensive field -> attacking source-cell qualification -> final lane offensive pressure`.
- P50 uses the Fort's **effective profile magnitude** as the offensive bonus source. It does not require an automatic defender to exist on the source cell; the Fort is projecting an offensive support field rather than reusing the conditional application of defensive pressure to a defender.
- Same-type overlapping Fort fields continue to use the baseline strongest-applicable same-type rule rather than stacking several P50 Fort bonuses.
- P50 applies from Forts owned by the holder as stated canonically; it must not silently inherit P18's broader self/fixed-teammate Fort qualification.

**Cross-type composition requirement**

- P50 does **not** require P51 in order to overlap another structure-derived offensive field. A baseline Command Post already projects offensive pressure, while P50 makes a Fort project offensive pressure.
- Where a P50 Fort offensive field and an ordinary/effective Command-Post offensive field both affect one engagement, their distinct cross-type bonuses use the catalogue's complement rule:

```text
combinedBonus = 1 - (1 - A) × (1 - B)
```

- Same-type strongest-field handling remains separate from this Fort+Command-Post cross-type composition.

**Explicit interactions**

- `P50 + P09`: P09 changes effective Fort coverage and effective defensive-pressure magnitude; P50 consumes both transformed values, so its offensive field changes on both axes.
- `P50 + N08`: N08 removes Fort defensive-pressure bonus. Because P50's offensive magnitude is defined as the Fort's effective defensive-pressure magnitude, P50's projected offensive magnitude becomes zero rather than restoring the removed value through a hidden baseline lookup.
- `P50 + N10`: N10 contracts effective Fort coverage and therefore contracts P50's offensive-support region.
- `P50 + P18`: both may add offensive pressure to one lane from Fort-related state but are distinct mechanics. P18 checks self/fixed-teammate Fort area and adds its own +100%; P50 checks the holder's owned active Fort profile and projects its effective magnitude. Representative multi-source pressure composition must preserve both.
- `P50 + P51`: explicitly legal reciprocal support. P50 adds Fort-derived offense while P51 adds Command-Post-derived defense; each direction uses the same Fort+Command-Post complement rule when both structure types contribute.
- Negative assertion against P03: P03 removes hostile Fort-derived **defensive** pressure when the P03 holder attacks. It must not erase P50 offensive support being projected by the P50 holder on that holder's own attacking lane.

**Non-effects**

- P50 does not create automatic defenders, make Forts autonomously attack, alter Fort cost/level/ownership, or modify Tank/Warship/strategic-weapon damage.

# 4. Emerging validation-boundary additions

P41–P50 reinforce the existing provisional boundaries and add several important shapes:

- **Structure purchase action shape** can itself be transformed (`P41`) independently of upgrade mechanics; P41 now has one canonical five-second fresh-construction state (`completedLevel = none`, inactive, target L5) followed by atomic L5 activation.
- **Cross-resource unit purchases** require atomic Population/economy/unit validation (`P42`).
- **Chassis transformation** should project one complete effective unit profile rather than scattered trait checks (`P43`).
- **Combat event -> territory/terrain mutation** is a first-class cross-domain seam (`P44`).
- **Observation/concealment** is now clearly its own validation domain, with terrain-driven (`P45`) and structure-driven (`P49`) masks feeding one lawful observation projection.
- **Terrain permission vs terrain identity** must remain separate (`P46`).
- **Post-capture side effects** need a dedicated capture-resolution seam rather than being folded into pressure (`P47`).
- **Faction-relative terrain classification** is a reusable core hook consumed by Capacity, Spawn quota accounting, terrain-share calculations, selectors/mechanics APIs, and cross-faction mechanics (`P48`).
- **Structure-profile-derived reciprocal combat fields** belong at the structure->combat seam (`P50`, P51).
- The interaction registry must support both **same-Origin interactions** and **cross-faction Origin interactions**, exemplified by `P44 attacker -> P48 defender`.
