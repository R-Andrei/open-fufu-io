# Open Fufu — Official AI Trait Support Rationale: P51–P54, N01–N06

## Status and authority

This is a canonical rationale shard for the trait-support catalogue defined by `OFFICIAL_AI_TRAIT_SUPPORT.md`.

Exact code-readable mappings for this range live in:

- `../design/official-ai/origin-trait-support.p51-n06.config.ts`

Game mechanics remain authoritative in `ORIGIN_TRAIT_CATALOGUE.md` and the relevant canonical gameplay documents. This file explains strategic intent and support boundaries only.

---

# P51 — One Flag Beneath the Stars

P51 makes Command Post coverage defensive as well as offensive. This is not just another defense scalar: one infrastructure geometry now supports pressure in both directions, so Command placement can become a genuine general-support decision.

Extended support lets Defense and Infrastructure reasoning value Command coverage as protection without changing the existing offensive role or granting the character any new preference for war.

**Strategic philosophy:** turn Command Posts into general-purpose battle-space support whose placement can strengthen both attack and defense.

---

# P52 — Humanity Has Declined

P52 converts unused Population Capacity into passive FFY. This creates a cross-domain relationship between territorial Capacity, Population expenditure/recovery, and economic income: gaining Capacity can have immediate economic value even before Population fills it, while Population recovery gradually reduces that bonus.

Extended support therefore teaches Economy/Forecast reasoning about the dynamic Capacity-minus-Population gap, Expansion reasoning about the extra value of acquiring Capacity, and Spending reasoning that Population expenditure can have an economic consequence without implying that the AI should waste Population merely to farm FFY.

**Strategic philosophy:** monetize underpopulation and excess Capacity, turning demographic slack into a temporary economic engine.

---

# P53 — Money Is Everything

P53 converts ready charges on persistent Missile Silos into passive income. A ready strategic weapon is therefore simultaneously military capability, deterrent capacity, and an income-producing stockpile. Firing a charge can create a real economic opportunity cost until it is restored.

Extended support lets Economy/Forecast reasoning value ready-charge income and lets Infrastructure/Upgrade/StrategicWeapon planning account for the economic value of Silo capacity and the income downtime caused by using a charge. P29 Warship launch capability is deliberately excluded because the mechanical trait itself excludes it.

**Strategic philosophy:** treat a ready strategic arsenal as productive capital, balancing launch value against the continuing income generated by keeping charges ready.

---

# P54 — Starlight Breaker

P54 changes the geometry of every generated Initial-Territory footprint into the accepted thin five-point star while leaving final territory count and Starting Population unchanged. This is structurally different from an ordinary compact start: candidate origins must be evaluated using the actual star footprint rather than a circular approximation.

Extended Spawn support therefore exposes the real footprint geometry and its likely additional frontage/exposure. The star may create useful reach in several directions, but it does not guarantee multiple theaters or valuable access, so no broad multi-theater affordance is asserted automatically.

P39 is an especially important global-synergy-sweep partner because the accepted combined mechanic produces two star-shaped split footprints.

**Strategic philosophy:** accept a more exposed, directional starting geometry in exchange for unusual reach and positional shape.

---

# N01 — The Lost Decade

N01 is a straightforward effective-mechanics drawback: Cities contribute less Population Growth. Shared Economy/Growth reasoning should already use the faction's real City growth contribution, so no trait-specific hook is needed.

The drawback intentionally declares no positive strategic theme or affordance. A competent controller may respond by relying less on City-driven growth or valuing alternative Population engines more highly, but that conclusion should emerge from ordinary effective-value comparison rather than a bespoke N01 doctrine.

**Strategic consequence:** City-centered demographic development is less productive.

---

# N02 — Flat Is Justice

N02 reduces offensive pressure on Plains. Shared LandWar/Territory reasoning already evaluates actual terrain-adjusted pressure, so this remains generic.

`TERRAIN_DEPENDENCE` records that the weakness is conditional on fighting from Plains rather than a global offensive penalty.

**Strategic consequence:** Plains are worse offensive staging terrain for this faction.

---

# N03 — I Hate Sand

N03 reduces defensive pressure on Desert. Shared Defense/Territory reasoning already sees the effective Desert pressure, so no special support is required.

`TERRAIN_DEPENDENCE` records that the weakness only matters where Desert geography is strategically relevant.

**Strategic consequence:** Desert is unusually poor defensive ground for this faction.

---

# N04 — Northern Expedition

N04 halves FFY events located on Mountain. Shared Economy/Territory reasoning should use the actual event-location multiplier, making Mountain-centered economic activity less attractive without requiring a dedicated avoidance planner.

**Strategic consequence:** economic events tied to Mountain geography are substantially less valuable.

---

# N05 — Curse of the Abyss

N05 is the first drawback in catalogue order that requires extended support. Fallout is not merely slower or less profitable territory for this faction: it is outright uncapturable. That can turn Fallout into a persistent ownership barrier that breaks otherwise plausible expansion routes, invasion plans, or reconnection paths.

Extended Territory/Threat/Forecast support therefore treats Fallout as faction-specific inaccessible ownership geometry, while Expansion/LandWar support prevents plans whose success depends on capturing Fallout. Ordinary legality remains authoritative; the hooks exist so higher-level geometry does not repeatedly plan through territory that the faction can never own.

P16, P35, P44, and any other Fallout-producing mechanics are mandatory global-synergy-sweep partners.

**Strategic consequence:** Fallout can become a permanent territorial barrier rather than merely difficult ground.

---

# N06 — No Second Season

N06 prohibits spending FFY on structure upgrades. The hard restriction should already be surfaced by normal legality and candidate generation, so the individual trait remains generic: Upgrade planning simply has no legal FFY-upgrade actions to produce.

The important issue is compositional rather than individual. P17 derives much of its strategic meaning from cheaper future upgrades; N06 can mechanically nullify that value. P41 may still create a level-5 City directly because that is a purchase rule rather than an FFY upgrade action. These interactions must be resolved explicitly during the global combination sweep.

This batch therefore records a required architecture audit for the combination phase: when one trait disables another trait's strategic affordance or support hook, the composed `OriginStrategicProfile` must not continue advertising mechanically impossible value. The generic support contract should only be amended once the full drawback catalogue reveals the complete set of suppression cases.

**Strategic consequence:** development must rely on initial purchases and other non-upgrade paths rather than ordinary FFY-funded modernization.

---

## Batch 6 consistency notes — P51–P54, N01–N06

P51–P54 and N05 require extended support. N01–N04 and N06 remain generic mechanics-aware cases.

No new shared theme, affordance, caution, or synergy-tag literal is required by this batch.

The first drawbacks expose a global-composition question that is intentionally deferred to the mandatory post-trait synergy sweep: some drawbacks may partially or completely suppress strategic semantics emitted by positive traits. P17 + N06 is the first clear example and must not be left as a contradictory composed profile.
